Muting

X's rules forbid "scripting the X website" and say: "The use of these techniques may result in the permanent suspension of your account." Muting scripts x.com. A lower daily number lowers the risk; no number makes it allowed.

Before turning this on, add accounts you never want muted to Always show, under Your accounts.

Muting starts again on the next UTC day. What each reason means

25 runs whenever the browser is open. 50 and 100 run only while x.com is open in a tab.

How muting works
  • Mutes up to the number a day you pick, one every few minutes, in background tabs it opens and closes.
  • Clicks X's own Mute on x.com, so the mutes reach the X apps too. It never uses X's API.
  • Mutes the list, your Also hide accounts and any bot set to Mute on X.
  • Skips accounts you follow, Always show accounts and every other bot, even one in Also hide.
  • Never unmutes. To undo a mute, unmute the account on X.

Bots that answer when tagged

A row you haven't changed follows the list: Hide here if the bot is on the list or in Also hide, Show if not. Mute on X hides the bot here and mutes it while muting is on.

Tag hiding works in this browser only. For the X apps too, add the bot's handle (such as @grok) as a muted word on X. To block a bot, open its profile from its name.

Your accounts

Export or import backs up these boxes and your bot choices, or adds a list someone shared.

Label replies

Slop adds the account to Also hide; Not slop adds it to Always show. Each label keeps the reply's text and link, the post it answers, the time, and whether the list or your boxes already had the account. Labels stay in this browser; nothing is sent anywhere. To label a hidden reply, pause hiding first.
